To delete your YouTube search history on your phone, **open the YouTube app, tap You, tap the gear icon, then tap Manage all history.** From there, you can delete your searches from the last hour, the last day, or all time. To remove just one search, tap the search bar, press and hold the search you want gone, and tap **Remove**.

The steps are almost the same on iPhone and Android. Below, you will learn how to delete one search, clear everything, stop YouTube from saving searches, and set your history to delete on its own.

## Why delete your YouTube search history?

There are a few good reasons:

- **Privacy.** Anyone who picks up your phone or shares your TV can see what you searched.
- **Better suggestions.** Old searches can fill your recommendations with videos you no longer want.
- **Gift surprises.** Searching for a gift idea on a shared account can spoil the surprise.
- **A fresh start.** Clearing history is an easy way to reset what YouTube shows you.

Your search history is saved to your **Google account**, not just your phone. So when you delete it on one device, it goes away on all your signed-in devices.

## How to delete one YouTube search

This is the fastest option if you only want to hide one or two searches.

1. Open the **YouTube** app.
2. Tap the **search** icon at the top.
3. Your past searches show up in a list. Find the one you want to remove.
4. **Press and hold** the search.
5. Tap **Remove** to confirm.

On some phones, you may see a small **X** or arrow next to each search instead. Tapping that also removes it.

## How to clear all your YouTube search history

1. Open the **YouTube** app.
2. Tap **You** at the bottom right.
3. Tap the **gear icon** at the top right to open **Settings**.
4. Tap **Manage all history**. This opens your YouTube history in your Google account.
5. Tap **Delete**.
6. Choose **Delete today**, **Delete custom range**, or **Delete all time**.
7. Confirm your choice.

This clears your search history and your watch history together. If you only want to remove searches, you can scroll through the list and delete single items by tapping the **X** next to each one.

## How to pause YouTube search history

If you do not want YouTube to save your searches at all, you can pause it.

1. Go to **Settings** and tap **Manage all history**, as shown above.
2. Tap the **Controls** tab at the top.
3. Find **YouTube History** and turn it **off**.
4. Confirm by tapping **Pause** or **Turn off**.

You can also choose to keep saving what you watch but not what you search. Look for the options to **include the videos you watch** and **include your searches**, and uncheck the one you do not want.

Keep in mind that pausing history makes your recommendations less personal. You can turn it back on at any time.

## How to set YouTube history to delete on its own

If you do not want to remember to clear your history, let YouTube do it for you.

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Manage all history**.
2. Tap the **Controls** tab.
3. Tap **Auto-delete**.
4. Choose to delete activity older than **3 months**, **18 months**, or **36 months**.
5. Tap **Next**, then **Confirm**.

YouTube will keep recent history for good suggestions and remove the old history for you.

## Use Incognito mode for private searches

If you want to search without saving anything just once, try **Incognito mode** in the YouTube app on many phones.

1. Tap your **profile picture** or **You**.
2. Tap **Turn on Incognito**.

While it is on, YouTube does not save your searches or the videos you watch. Tap the Incognito icon and turn it off when you are done. Incognito mode turns off on its own after a while if you stop using the app.

## Delete YouTube history from a web browser

You can also manage everything from any browser on your phone or computer.

1. Go to **myactivity.google.com** and sign in.
2. Tap **YouTube History**.
3. Tap **Delete** and choose a time range, or delete single searches from the list.

If you also want to clear the history in your web browser, not just YouTube, clearing browser data can help. It can even speed things up. See our tips on [making Google Chrome faster](/why-is-google-chrome-so-slow).

## Quick reference

| What you want | Where to go |
|---|---|
| Remove one search | Search bar, press and hold, Remove |
| Clear all history | You, Settings, Manage all history, Delete |
| Stop saving searches | Manage all history, Controls, turn off YouTube History |
| Auto-delete old history | Manage all history, Controls, Auto-delete |
| Search privately once | Profile, Turn on Incognito |

## What about YouTube on a shared TV or tablet?

If your whole family uses one YouTube account, everyone sees the same history. A few ideas can help:

- **Add separate accounts.** Each person can sign in with their own Google account on the TV or tablet.
- **Use YouTube Kids** for young children. It has its own settings.
- **Pause history** on the shared account so searches are not saved.

## Free up space while you are at it

Clearing your history does not free up much space on your phone. But the YouTube app's cache and downloaded videos can take up a lot. On Android, go to **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **YouTube**, then **Storage**, and tap **Clear cache**. In the YouTube app, you can also delete downloaded videos from the **Downloads** section. ## Search history vs watch history

YouTube keeps two separate lists:

| | Search history | Watch history |
|---|---|---|
| What it saves | The words you type in the search bar | The videos you play, even for a few seconds |
| Shows up in | Search suggestions | The History page and your recommendations |
| Can you delete it? | Yes | Yes |
| Can you pause it? | Yes | Yes |

Clearing your searches does not remove the videos you watched. If you want a full clean start, clear both.

## What happens if you pause watch history?

Pausing watch history affects more than your history page. YouTube has said that if your watch history is off and you have no past history to use, the **home page will not show recommended videos**. You will see the search bar and a message about your history instead.

This is great if you want fewer distractions. If you want your recommendations back, turn watch history on again and watch a few videos you like.

## Reset recommendations without deleting everything

If your feed is full of videos you do not want, you do not have to wipe your whole history:

- Tap the **three dots** next to a video on the home page and choose **Not interested**.
- Choose **Don't recommend channel** to block a whole channel from your feed.
- Delete just the few videos from your watch history that are causing the bad suggestions.

## The bottom line

To delete your YouTube search history on your phone, go to **You**, then **Settings**, then **Manage all history**, and tap **Delete**. To remove one search, press and hold it in the search bar and tap **Remove**. You can also pause your history or set it to delete on its own after 3, 18, or 36 months. Since your history is saved to your Google account, the change applies to all your devices.
