To cancel your Costco membership, **go to the membership counter at any Costco warehouse or call Costco at 1-800-774-2678.** Costco does not let you cancel online. The primary member should cancel, and you will usually get a **full refund** of your membership fee. If you have an Executive membership, any 2% reward you earned is taken out of that refund.

Canceling only takes a few minutes. This guide shows both ways to cancel, how the refund works, and what to do if you just want to stop auto-renewal.

## Before you cancel: what you need

- **Your membership card** or membership number
- **A photo ID** if you cancel in person
- **The primary member.** The person whose name is first on the account should cancel.
- **Any unused Executive rewards.** Think about spending your 2% reward certificate first, since it will be taken out of your refund.

## Option 1: Cancel at a Costco warehouse

This is the fastest way to get your refund.

1. Go to the **membership counter** near the entrance of any Costco warehouse. It does not have to be the store where you signed up.
2. Tell the staff you want to **cancel your membership**.
3. Show your **membership card** and **photo ID**.
4. The staff will process the cancellation and give you a **refund**, often right away.

Your refund usually goes back to the card you paid with, or you may get cash or a store credit, depending on how you paid.

## Option 2: Cancel by phone

1. Call Costco customer service at **1-800-774-2678**.
2. Choose the option for **membership** questions.
3. Tell the agent you want to cancel.
4. Give your **membership number** and answer a few questions to confirm who you are.

Costco's phone system has noted that you should call **after your membership fee has posted** to your account. If you just renewed and the fee is still pending, wait a few days before calling.

## How the Costco refund works

Costco's membership comes with a satisfaction guarantee. Here is how it works at the time of writing.

| Membership | Yearly fee | Refund when you cancel |
|---|---|---|
| Gold Star | $65 | Full fee refunded |
| Business | $65 | Full fee refunded |
| Executive | $130 | Full fee, minus any 2% reward you earned |

Costco lets you cancel at any time during your membership year. For Executive members, Costco says any 2% reward that has been issued or earned is subtracted from the refund.

**Example:** You paid $130 for Executive and earned $40 in 2% rewards. If you cancel, your refund would be about $90.

## Want to downgrade instead?

If Costco is still worth it but the Executive fee is not, you can **downgrade to Gold Star**.

- Go to the membership counter or call customer service.
- Ask to switch from Executive to Gold Star.
- Costco refunds the extra upgrade fee, minus any 2% reward you already earned.

Not sure which one is right for you? Our guide on [whether a Costco membership is worth the fee](/is-costco-membership-worth-it) walks through the math.

## How to turn off auto-renewal

If you just do not want to be charged next year, you can turn off auto-renewal. Your membership stays active until it ends.

1. Sign in at **costco.com**.
2. Go to **Account**, then **Membership**.
3. Find **Auto Renewal** and turn it off.

This does **not** cancel your current membership or give you a refund. It just stops the next charge.

## What happens after you cancel?

- Your membership card stops working right away.
- Any **household card** on your account stops working too.
- You can no longer shop in the warehouse, but you may still be able to buy some items on costco.com (often with an added fee for non-members).
- You can **rejoin later** if you change your mind.

## Tips before you cancel

- **Use your 2% reward first.** If you are an Executive member, spend your reward certificate before you cancel.
- **Think about downgrading.** Gold Star costs half as much as Executive.
- **Finish returns.** Costco's return policy is generous, but you may want to return items while you are still a member.
- **Check other subscriptions.** Some deals, like Costco Travel bookings, may be linked to your membership.

## Real refund examples

These examples show how the refund usually works. Costco's rules can change, so confirm with Costco before you cancel.

| Situation | Refund you might get |
|---|---|
| Gold Star member, canceled after 3 months | About $65 (the full fee) |
| Gold Star member, canceled after 11 months | About $65 (the full fee) |
| Executive member, earned $20 in 2% rewards | About $110 ($130 minus $20) |
| Executive member, earned $150 in 2% rewards | About $0 from the fee, since the reward is more than the refund |
| Executive member downgrading to Gold Star, earned $30 | About $35 back ($65 upgrade fee minus $30) |

## What to say when you cancel

You do not need a long explanation. You can say:

> "Hi, I'd like to cancel my membership and get a refund, please. I'm the primary member, and here is my card and ID."

If you only want to switch plans, say:

> "I'd like to downgrade from Executive to Gold Star, please."

Staff may ask why you are leaving. You can give a short reason or simply say it is not a fit right now.

## Canceling a household or business card

- **Household cards:** The free household card is tied to the primary member. When the main membership is canceled, the household card stops working too.
- **Business memberships:** The business owner or primary member usually needs to cancel. Extra cardholders on a business account can often be removed without closing the whole membership.

## Things to do before you cancel

- **Use your Executive reward certificate** if you have one.
- **Finish any big returns** you are still planning to make.
- **Check gift cards and Costco Shop Cards,** which you may still be able to use online.
- **Use up prescriptions or optical orders** in progress, or ask how canceling affects them.
- **Cancel Costco Travel or services** linked to your membership, like auto programs, if needed.
- **Turn off auto-renew online** so a new fee is not charged while you are deciding.

## Cancel vs downgrade vs let it expire

| Choice | What happens | Good if |
|---|---|---|
| Cancel now | Refund of the fee, card stops working today | You are sure you are done with Costco |
| Downgrade | Keep shopping, get back the upgrade fee minus rewards | Executive is too much, but Costco still helps |
| Turn off auto-renew | Keep shopping until the end of your year, no refund | You want to finish the year first |

## If you move somewhere without a Costco

If you are moving to an area with no nearby warehouse, you can still cancel by phone. You may also be able to keep shopping on costco.com, where some items ship to you, but think about whether that is worth the fee.

## The bottom line

You cannot cancel a Costco membership online. Go to the membership counter at any warehouse with your card and photo ID, or call 1-800-774-2678. Costco usually refunds your full fee, but Executive members lose any 2% reward they earned from the refund. If you only want to stop next year's charge, turn off auto-renewal on costco.com instead.
