To view saved Reels on Facebook, **open the app, tap the Menu button, then tap Saved.** All the Reels, posts, and videos you saved are there. You can also open the **Reels** tab, tap your profile icon or the three dots at the top, and tap **Saved** to see only your saved Reels.

Facebook moves its buttons around from time to time, so the exact look may be a little different on your phone. The steps below cover iPhone, Android, and a computer, plus how to find Reels you liked or watched but did not save.

## How to view saved Reels on iPhone and Android

The Facebook app works almost the same way on iPhone and Android.

### Method 1: From the Menu

1. Open the **Facebook** app.
2. Tap the **Menu** button. On iPhone it is at the bottom right. On Android it is at the top right. It looks like three lines or your profile picture.
3. Tap **Saved**. If you do not see it, tap **See more** first.
4. Tap the **filter** or **All** button at the top and pick **Reels** to show only Reels.

### Method 2: From the Reels tab

1. Open the **Facebook** app.
2. Tap the **Reels** or **Video** tab.
3. Tap your **profile icon** or the **three dots** at the top right.
4. Tap **Saved**. On some older app versions, you tap **See all** next to "Recently saved."

Both methods show the same saved Reels. Use whichever one is easier for you.

## How to view saved Reels on a computer

1. Go to **facebook.com** in any web browser and log in.
2. On the left side of the page, click **Saved**. If you do not see it, click **See more**.
3. Click the **filter** or **More** button at the top right of your saved list.
4. Choose **Reels** to show only saved Reels.

Your saved list is linked to your account, so Reels you save on your phone also show up on your computer.

## How to save a Reel so you can find it later

If you want to keep a Reel to watch again, save it:

1. While the Reel is playing, tap the **three dots** on the right side or bottom.
2. Tap **Save reel** or **Save video**.

It goes straight to your Saved list. You can also add it to a **collection**, which is like a folder. Collections help when you save a lot, for example "Recipes," "Workouts," or "Funny."

## How to find Reels you liked but did not save

Facebook keeps a record of the Reels you liked in your **Activity log**.

1. Tap the **Menu** button.
2. Tap **Settings and privacy**, then **Settings**.
3. Scroll to **Activity log** and tap it.
4. Look for **Your interactions** or **Likes and reactions**.
5. Scroll through the list, or use the filter to show only videos.

This list can be long, so it helps to know roughly when you liked the Reel.

## How to find Reels you watched but did not save or like

This is harder. Facebook does not keep a simple list of every Reel you watched. Here are some things to try:

- **Check your video watch history.** Some versions of the app have a **Watch history** or **Videos you've watched** section under the Video tab or in the Activity log.
- **Search for the creator.** If you remember who posted it, go to their profile and tap **Reels**.
- **Search for key words.** Type words you remember from the Reel into the Facebook search bar and tap the **Reels** filter.
- **Check your messages.** If a friend sent you the Reel, it will still be in your Messenger chat.

Next time, save any Reel you might want again. It takes one tap and saves a lot of searching.

## Why your saved Reels are missing

If a Reel you saved is gone, one of these is usually the reason.

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The creator deleted the Reel | It cannot be recovered |
| The creator made it private | Only people they allow can see it now |
| Your app is out of date | Update Facebook in the App Store or Google Play |
| You are on a different account | Check which account you are logged in to |
| The app is glitching | Close and reopen the app, or check on a computer |
| Weak internet | Saved Reels will not load on a slow connection |

If Reels will not load at all, the problem may be your connection. Our guide on [fixing slow internet at home](/why-is-my-internet-so-slow) can help.

## Clear up space if Facebook is slow

Watching lots of Reels fills up your phone with cached video files. If Facebook feels slow or your phone says it is almost full, clearing the app's cache can help.

- **On Android:** Go to **Settings**, then **Apps**, then **Facebook**, then **Storage**, and tap **Clear cache**.
- **On iPhone:** Facebook does not have a clear cache button. You can go to **Settings**, then **General**, then **iPhone Storage**, then **Facebook**, and tap **Offload App**, then reinstall it.

Your saved Reels will not be deleted. They are saved to your account, not your phone. For more ways to free up space, see [what to do when phone storage is full](/why-is-my-phone-storage-full).

## Tips to keep your saved Reels organized

- **Use collections.** Sort Reels into groups like "Recipes" or "Home tips" as you save them.
- **Clean out your list.** Unsave Reels you no longer need so the good ones are easier to find.
- **Save the creator too.** Follow creators you like so you can find their Reels quickly.
- **Share it with yourself.** Sending a Reel to yourself in Messenger is another easy way to keep it.

If you also watch a lot of videos on YouTube and want to keep your history private, see our guide on [how to delete YouTube search history on your phone](/delete-youtube-search-history-on-your-phone).

## How to share a saved Reel

1. Open your **Saved** list and tap the Reel.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the arrow icon).
3. Choose where to send it, like Messenger, WhatsApp, a text message, or **Copy link**.

The person you send it to can watch it as long as the Reel is public and still posted.

## Can you download a saved Reel?

Facebook only lets you download **your own** Reels. To save one you made, open the Reel, tap the **three dots**, and look for **Download** or **Save video**.

Facebook does not offer a download button for other people's Reels. Be careful with third-party "Reel downloader" websites and apps. Some are full of ads, ask for your Facebook login, or install unwanted software. The safest way to keep someone else's Reel is to leave it in your Saved list or send the link to yourself.

## Why a saved Reel can disappear for good

Your Saved list only holds a link to the Reel, not a copy. So a Reel will vanish from your list if:

- The person who posted it **deletes** it
- They change who can see it, like **Friends only** or **Only me**
- Facebook **removes** it for breaking its rules
- The account that posted it is **deleted or blocked**

If a Reel matters to you, like a recipe or how-to, write down the key steps or the creator's name so you can find it again.

## The bottom line

Your saved Facebook Reels are in the **Saved** section. On the app, tap **Menu**, then **Saved**, and filter by Reels. On a computer, click **Saved** on the left side. If you liked a Reel but did not save it, look in your **Activity log**. And if a saved Reel is missing, the creator may have deleted it or made it private.
