To sign up for Walmart+, **go to walmart.com/plus or open the Walmart app, tap Start free trial, sign in, and add a payment card.** Your membership starts right away, and most people get a **30-day free trial** first. After the trial, Walmart+ costs **$12.95 a month or $98 a year** at the time of writing, plus tax.

The whole process takes about 2 minutes. This guide walks you through each step, explains what you get, and helps you decide if it is worth the money.

## What is Walmart+?

Walmart+ is a paid membership, a bit like Amazon Prime. Members get perks like free grocery delivery, free shipping, gas discounts, and a free streaming service. You pay a monthly or yearly fee to get these benefits.

## Walmart+ cost at a glance

| Plan | Price | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $12.95 per month | Try it without a big payment |
| Yearly | $98 per year | Best value if you plan to keep it |
| Walmart+ Assist | About $6.47 per month or $49 per year | People who get SNAP, Medicaid, or other qualifying aid |

Paying yearly costs about **$8.17 a month**, which saves you about $57 a year compared to the monthly plan. Walmart sometimes runs sales on the yearly plan, especially around big shopping events. Prices can change, so always check the current price before you sign up.

## How to sign up for Walmart+ on the app

1. Open the **Walmart** app. Download it from the App Store or Google Play if you do not have it.
2. Sign in to your Walmart account, or create one with your email.
3. Tap the **Walmart+** banner on the home screen, or tap **Account** and then **Walmart+**.
4. Tap **Start free trial** or **Join Walmart+**.
5. Choose **monthly** or **yearly** billing.
6. Add or select a **payment card**.
7. Review the terms and tap **Start free trial** or **Join**.

You will get a confirmation email, and the Walmart+ benefits show up in your app right away.

## How to sign up for Walmart+ on a computer

1. Go to **walmart.com/plus** in your web browser.
2. Click **Start free trial**.
3. Sign in or create a Walmart account.
4. Choose monthly or yearly billing.
5. Enter your payment details.
6. Click **Start free trial** to finish.

## How the free trial works

- The trial usually lasts **30 days**.
- You get most member benefits during the trial. Walmart says a few perks are only for paid members.
- You need to add a card, even though you will not be charged during the trial.
- When the trial ends, you are charged automatically for the plan you picked.
- If you do not want to pay, **cancel before the trial ends**. Set a reminder on your phone for a day or two before.

## Main Walmart+ benefits

Here are the benefits most people use. Walmart adds and changes perks from time to time, so check the Walmart+ page for the latest list.

- **Free delivery from your store** on orders of $35 or more. Orders under $35 have a small fee.
- **Free shipping** on items shipped from walmart.com, with no order minimum. Some large or heavy items are left out.
- **Gas savings** of up to 10 cents per gallon at participating Exxon, Mobil, Walmart, and Murphy stations.
- **A free streaming service.** Members can choose Paramount+ Essential or Peacock Premium.
- **Scan and Go** in the Walmart app, so you can scan items as you shop and pay without waiting in line.
- **Early access to deals** during big sales events.
- **Free returns from home** on many items.

## How to use your delivery benefit

1. In the Walmart app, pick **Delivery** at the top.
2. Choose a delivery time slot.
3. Add at least $35 of items to your cart.
4. Check out. The delivery fee should show as free.

You can add a tip for your driver when you check out. Tips are optional but a nice way to say thanks. If you are not sure how much to give, our guide on [how to work out a tip](/how-to-calculate-a-tip) makes it easy.

## Is Walmart+ worth it?

It depends on how you shop. A simple way to decide is to add up what you would save in a month.

| If you... | You might save about |
|---|---|
| Get grocery delivery 2 times a month | $15 to $20 in delivery fees |
| Fill up a 15-gallon tank 4 times a month | About $6 in gas |
| Use the streaming service instead of paying for it | $8 to $11 |

If your savings add up to more than $8 to $13 a month, Walmart+ likely pays for itself. If you only visit Walmart once in a while and do not use delivery, it may not be worth it.

**Good fit if you:**

- Order groceries online often
- Live near a Walmart or member gas station
- Already want Paramount+ or Peacock
- Order a lot of small items online

**Maybe skip it if you:**

- Rarely shop at Walmart
- Prefer to pick up groceries yourself (pickup is often free anyway)
- Already pay for another delivery membership you use more

## How to cancel Walmart+

1. Sign in to your account on the **Walmart app** or **walmart.com**.
2. Go to **Account**, then **Walmart+**.
3. Tap **Manage membership**.
4. Tap **Cancel membership** and follow the steps.

You can usually keep using your benefits until the end of the time you already paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you should not be charged.

## Tips to get the most from Walmart+

- **Link your streaming account** in the Walmart app so you do not miss that perk.
- **Use the gas discount** by opening the Walmart app at the pump.
- **Plan bigger orders** to reach the $35 delivery minimum.
- **Watch for yearly plan sales** if you know you will keep it.
- **Check your email** for member-only deals.

## The bottom line

Signing up for Walmart+ takes just a few minutes on the app or at walmart.com/plus. Start with the 30-day free trial, try the free delivery and gas savings, and see if you use it enough. At $12.95 a month or $98 a year, it is a good deal for people who order groceries often, and easy to cancel if it is not the right fit.
