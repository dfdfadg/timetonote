Zoom error code 10004 means **the Zoom app could not reach Zoom's servers to check for or install an update.** To fix it, **check Zoom's service status, make sure your internet works, allow Zoom through your firewall or antivirus, and reinstall the latest version from Zoom's website.** One of these steps fixes the error for most people.

If you have a meeting right now, you can usually still join from a web browser at zoom.us or from the Zoom app on your phone while you fix your computer.

## 7 ways to fix Zoom error code 10004

### 1. Check if Zoom is down

Go to **status.zoom.us**. If Zoom shows an outage, the error is on their end. Wait a little while and try again.

### 2. Check your internet connection

- Open a website to make sure you are online.
- Restart your router and modem.
- Try a different network, like your phone's hotspot.

If your connection is slow or keeps dropping, see our guides on [fixing slow internet](/why-is-my-internet-so-slow) and [Wi-Fi that will not connect](/why-is-my-wifi-not-working).

### 3. Restart Zoom and your computer

Quit Zoom fully, then restart your computer. On Windows, right-click the Zoom icon near the clock and choose **Exit**. On Mac, choose **Zoom** in the menu bar, then **Quit**.

### 4. Allow Zoom through your firewall

**On Windows:**

1. Open **Windows Security**, then **Firewall & network protection**.
2. Click **Allow an app through firewall**.
3. Click **Change settings**, find **Zoom**, and check both **Private** and **Public**.
4. Click **OK** and open Zoom again.

**On Mac:** Go to **System Settings**, then **Network**, then **Firewall**, then **Options**, and make sure Zoom is allowed to receive connections.

### 5. Pause antivirus or VPN for a moment

Some antivirus programs and VPNs block Zoom's update servers. Pause them briefly, try the update, then turn them back on. If that fixes it, add Zoom to your antivirus's allowed list.

### 6. Update Zoom manually

In the Zoom app, click your **profile picture**, then **Check for Updates**. If that fails with 10004, go to the next step.

### 7. Uninstall and reinstall Zoom

1. **Uninstall Zoom.** On Windows, go to **Settings**, then **Apps**, find Zoom, and click **Uninstall**. On Mac, open Zoom, click **zoom.us** in the menu bar, and choose **Uninstall Zoom**.
2. **Restart** your computer.
3. **Download the latest version** from the official Zoom download page at zoom.us/download.
4. **Install it** and sign in.

Your meetings and settings are saved to your account, so you will not lose them.

## Quick checklist

| Check | Fix |
|---|---|
| Is Zoom down? | Check status.zoom.us and wait |
| Is the internet working? | Restart router, try another network |
| Firewall blocking Zoom? | Allow Zoom in firewall settings |
| Antivirus or VPN blocking it? | Pause it, then allow Zoom |
| Old version stuck? | Uninstall and reinstall from zoom.us |
| Work or school computer? | Ask your IT team, since they may block updates |

## On a work or school computer?

Many offices and schools control software updates. If you cannot change firewall settings or install software, contact your IT help desk. They may need to update Zoom for you.

## Is your computer slow too?

If Zoom freezes or lags even after the fix, your computer may be short on memory. Our guide on [making a slow laptop faster](/why-is-my-laptop-so-slow) has easy tips.

## The bottom line

Zoom error code 10004 means the app could not reach Zoom's update servers. Check status.zoom.us, test your internet, and allow Zoom through your firewall, antivirus, and VPN. If it still fails, uninstall Zoom and download the latest version from zoom.us. Your meetings and settings will be waiting when you sign back in.
