If your Amazon package says delivered but is not there, **check the delivery photo in Your Orders, look around your home and with neighbors, and wait up to 48 hours.** Amazon says packages are sometimes marked as delivered up to two days before they actually show up. If it still has not arrived after 48 hours, contact Amazon for a refund or replacement.

Most "missing" packages turn up. They are hidden behind a planter, left at a side door, given to a neighbor, or still on the truck. The steps below help you find it fast, and tell you exactly what to do if it is really gone.

## Step 1: Check the tracking details and photo

1. Open the **Amazon** app or website.
2. Go to **Your Orders** and find the package.
3. Tap **Track package**.

Look for:

- **A delivery photo.** Many drivers take a picture of where they left the box. Does it show your door?
- **Where it was left.** The tracking may say "front porch," "mailroom," "handed to resident," or "left in parcel locker."
- **The carrier.** Some Amazon orders are delivered by USPS, UPS, or FedEx. Their tracking may have more details.

If the photo shows a different door or house, the package was likely delivered to the wrong address. Take a screenshot.

## Step 2: Search around your home

Drivers sometimes leave boxes in spots you would not think to check.

- Behind bushes, planters, or porch furniture
- At a back door, side door, or garage
- Inside your mailbox (small packages)
- In your building's mailroom, front desk, or package locker
- With your landlord or apartment office

## Step 3: Ask your neighbors and household

- Ask people you live with if anyone brought it inside.
- Check with neighbors on both sides and across the street. Packages often go to the house next door.
- Look at a neighborhood app or group to see if someone found a box with your name on it.

## Step 4: Check your delivery address and instructions

Go to **Your Account**, then **Your Addresses**. Make sure the address on the order is right. Also check your **delivery instructions**, since you may have told drivers to leave packages somewhere specific, like a back porch.

## Step 5: Wait up to 48 hours

Amazon says a package can be marked delivered **up to 48 hours before it arrives.** This happens when a driver scans it too early or it gets mixed into the next day's route. Many packages show up the next day.

## Step 6: Contact Amazon

If 48 hours have passed and the package still is not there, report it.

1. Go to **Your Orders**.
2. Find the order and tap **Get help with order** or **Problem with order**.
3. Choose **Package didn't arrive** or a similar option.
4. Follow the steps to ask for a **refund** or **replacement**.

You can also chat with customer service in the Amazon app. Have your order number and any screenshots ready.

### If the item was sold by a third-party seller

Check the order page to see who sold and shipped it.

- **Shipped by Amazon:** Amazon handles the problem directly.
- **Shipped by the seller:** Contact the seller first through Your Orders. If they do not help within a couple of days, you can file a claim under Amazon's **A-to-z Guarantee**.

## What if Amazon will not help?

This is rare, but it can happen, especially if you report many missing packages.

- **Ask politely to speak with a supervisor.**
- **Dispute the charge with your credit card company.** Many cards protect you when you pay for something you never received.
- **Check your insurance.** Homeowners or renters insurance may cover theft of expensive items, though your deductible may be higher than the item's value.

## Was it stolen? What to do

If a doorbell camera shows someone taking the box, or you are sure it was stolen:

1. **Save the video or photos.**
2. **Report it to Amazon** as shown above.
3. **File a police report,** especially for expensive items. Some police departments let you report online.
4. **Warn your neighbors,** since package thieves often hit several homes on the same street.

## How to stop missing packages in the future

| Option | How it helps |
|---|---|
| Amazon Locker or Counter | Pick up your package from a secure spot near you |
| Delivery instructions | Tell drivers to leave boxes out of sight, like a side door |
| Doorbell camera | Helps prevent theft and gives you proof |
| Lockbox on your porch | Keeps boxes locked until you get home |
| Deliver to work | If your job allows packages |
| Pick a delivery day | Choose a day when you are home |
| Require a signature | Useful for expensive orders, if offered |

Also, check your tracking and bring packages inside as soon as you can.

## The bottom line

When Amazon says a package was delivered but you cannot find it, do not panic. Check the delivery photo, search around your home, ask neighbors, and wait up to 48 hours. If it still is not there, go to Your Orders and report it for a refund or replacement. To stop it from happening again, try an Amazon Locker, clear delivery instructions, or a porch lockbox.
