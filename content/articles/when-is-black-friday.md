**Black Friday 2026 is on Friday, November 27, 2026.** It is always the day after Thanksgiving in the United States. **Cyber Monday 2026 is on Monday, November 30.** Many stores start their deals weeks earlier, often in late October or early November.

This guide gives you all the key dates, explains when the best deals usually show up, and shares simple tips so you save money and avoid scams.

## Key holiday shopping dates for 2026

| Day | Date |
|---|---|
| Thanksgiving | Thursday, November 26, 2026 |
| **Black Friday** | **Friday, November 27, 2026** |
| Small Business Saturday | Saturday, November 28, 2026 |
| **Cyber Monday** | **Monday, November 30, 2026** |

## When do Black Friday deals really start?

Black Friday is no longer just one day. Here is what usually happens:

- **Late October and early November:** Many big retailers post "early Black Friday" deals. Some are just as good as the real day.
- **The week before Thanksgiving:** More deals go live, especially online.
- **Thanksgiving night and Black Friday:** Doorbuster deals and the deepest discounts on some items.
- **The weekend:** Many deals continue, and small shops run their own sales on Small Business Saturday.
- **Cyber Monday:** Online deals, often on clothing, tech, and subscriptions.

Membership programs sometimes give members early access. For example, Walmart+ members have gotten early access to some deals. Our [Walmart+ membership guide](/how-to-sign-up-for-walmart-plus) explains the perks.

## What to buy on Black Friday

Black Friday is often a good time for:

- **TVs** and streaming devices
- **Laptops, tablets, and headphones**
- **Kitchen appliances** like air fryers, blenders, and coffee makers
- **Vacuums** and robot vacuums
- **Smart home devices** like doorbells and speakers
- **Toys** and video games

It is often better to wait on:

- **Winter clothing and coats**, which usually get cheaper after the holidays
- **Mattresses and furniture**, which often have big sales around holiday weekends in spring and summer too
- **The newest phones**, which rarely get big direct discounts

## How to tell if a deal is real

Not every "sale" is a good deal. Before you buy:

1. **Check the price history.** A price tracker can show if the "sale" price is really lower than usual.
2. **Compare stores.** Check the same item at two or three retailers.
3. **Match the model number.** Some holiday deals are special models with fewer features.
4. **Work out the real discount.** Our [free percentage calculator](/tools/percentage-calculator) shows how much you are really saving.
5. **Read the return policy.** Some stores have shorter return windows for sale items.

## Stay safe from Black Friday scams

The FTC warns that scammers are busy during holiday shopping season. To stay safe:

- **Pay with a credit card.** It usually gives you more protection if something goes wrong.
- **Never pay with gift cards, wire transfers, or crypto** for an online order. The FTC says only scammers ask for these.
- **Be careful with very low prices** on social media ads. Scammers often pretend to be real brands.
- **Search the store's name** with words like "review," "complaint," or "scam" before you buy.
- **Watch for fake delivery texts.** Do not click links in unexpected messages about packages.

If an order never shows up, our guide on [what to do when a package says delivered but is missing](/amazon-package-says-delivered-but-not-here) can help.

## Simple tips to save more

- **Make a list and a budget** before the deals start.
- **Sign up for store emails** a few weeks early to see deals first.
- **Use cash back** from your credit card or shopping apps.
- **Stack deals** with store coupons when allowed.
- **Shop warehouse clubs** for bulk gifts and holiday food. See if [a Costco membership is worth it](/is-costco-membership-worth-it) for you.
- **Do not buy just because it is on sale.** A deal is only a deal if you needed it.

## Black Friday shopping plan: week by week

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Early October | Make a gift list and set a total budget |
| Mid October | Start tracking prices on the items you want |
| Early November | Sign up for store emails and apps, compare early deals |
| Week of Thanksgiving | Check final deals, buy items that hit your target price |
| Black Friday | Shop early for doorbusters, or buy online |
| Cyber Monday | Look for online-only deals and anything you missed |
| December | Watch for price drops and use price-match policies if offered |

## How to track prices

- **Browser extensions and price trackers** can show a product's price history and send alerts when it drops.
- **Store apps** often show member-only deals and let you save items to a list.
- **Write down the regular price** for your top items in October, so you know a real deal when you see it.

## Shipping deadlines matter

If you are buying gifts online, check each store's holiday shipping deadlines. Popular carriers and stores post their last dates for standard and express shipping in November and December. Ordering early in the season gives you time to return or exchange items that do not work out.

## Know the return policy

Many stores offer **extended holiday return windows** for items bought in November and December. But some electronics and sale items have shorter return periods. Before you buy:

- Check how many days you have to return the item.
- Keep receipts and order emails in one folder.
- Find out if you pay for return shipping.

## Black Friday vs other big sales

| Sale | Usually | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Prime Day | July, plus a fall event | Amazon devices, many household items |
| Labor Day | Early September | Mattresses, appliances |
| Black Friday | Day after Thanksgiving | TVs, electronics, toys, kitchen items |
| Cyber Monday | Monday after Thanksgiving | Online deals, clothing, tech |
| After-Christmas sales | December 26 and later | Decorations, winter clothes, gift sets |

If an item is not on sale on Black Friday, it may show up cheaper at one of these other times.

## The bottom line

Black Friday 2026 is on **Friday, November 27**, and Cyber Monday is on **Monday, November 30**. Many deals start weeks earlier, so start watching prices in late October. Check price history, compare stores, pay with a credit card, and skip deals that look too good to be true. With a list and a budget, you can save real money this holiday season.
